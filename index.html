Comparative Firewall Effectiveness Against Port Scanning Attacks on Linux Systems

Research Methodology in Computer Science

Conducted a comparative security research evaluating UFW (Uncomplicated Firewall) and Port Knocking against TCP SYN port scanning attacks on an Ubuntu 22.04 LTS server. Designed and executed a controlled experiment using Kali Linux/Nmap as the attacker environment across three defense scenarios (no protection, UFW, Port Knocking), measuring open ports, packet drop rate, CPU/RAM overhead, and scan response time. Applied Paired T-test statistical analysis (p<0.05) to validate significance of results, and presented findings on trade-offs between security effectiveness and performance cost for production and high-security use cases.
